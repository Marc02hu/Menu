Restaurant Menu

Prices are listed in Canadian dollars. Mild and medium spice levels follow the original menu. Gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan labels are supplied by the original menu. Please confirm ingredients, dietary suitability, and cross-contact risks with restaurant staff.

Soups and Noodle Soups

Can You Tantan?

Price: $16 CAD

A mildly spicy ramen noodle soup with ground chicken, pickled daikon radish, bok choy, scallions, and chilies in broth. The bowl combines noodles and chicken with leafy vegetables and pickled radish.

Spice level: mild.

U-Don Good

Price: $15 CAD

Udon noodles in vegetable broth with spicy tofu, mushrooms, eggplant, and bamboo shoots. This plant-based noodle soup combines tofu with several vegetables in a mildly spicy broth-based dish.

Marked vegan on the original menu. Spice level: mild.

Shanghai Moon

Price: $16 CAD

A broth-based soup with Shanghai noodles, pork dumplings, mushrooms, and gai lan, also known as Chinese broccoli. Crispy chicken skins add a contrasting texture. This dish includes both pork and chicken.

Spicy Beef Ramen

Price: $16 CAD

Ramen noodles served in spicy broth with beef brisket, gai lan, and corn. The beef and noodles are accompanied by leafy Chinese broccoli and kernels of corn.

Spice level: medium.

Sho-Me the Ramen

Price: $16 CAD

Ramen noodles in miso broth with sliced char siu pork, mushrooms, and bok choy. Miso provides a savoury fermented-soybean base for the noodles, sliced pork, and vegetables.

Spicy Seafood Soup

Price: $17 CAD

Rice noodles in laksa coconut broth with mussels, bay scallops, and tiger shrimp. Gai lan, scallions, cilantro, and lotus root chips accompany the seafood. The coconut-based broth is paired with a mild spice level.

Marked gluten-free on the original menu. Spice level: mild. Includes shellfish.

The Hop Bop

Price: $16 CAD

Rice noodles with barbecue pulled pork, lemongrass, bean sprouts, and Thai basil, served in shrimp paste broth. This soup combines pork with a seafood-based broth and aromatic herbs.

Spice level: mild. Includes shrimp paste.

Stir-Fry

Spicy Beef Stir Fry

Price: $16 CAD

A beef stir-fry with rice noodles, mushrooms, chilies, cashews, scallions, and sesame seeds. This dish brings together beef, noodles, vegetables, and nut and seed toppings.

Spice level: medium. The dish name specifies beef, although beef is not repeated in the original ingredient list. Please confirm the ingredients with staff.

Seafood Stir Fry

Price: $17 CAD

Udon noodles stir-fried with tiger shrimp, mussels, scallops, and seasonal vegetables in spicy hoisin sauce. This noodle dish contains a mixture of shellfish rather than a single seafood ingredient.

Spice level: mild. Includes shellfish.

Chicken and Veggie Stir Fry

Price: $16 CAD

Shanghai noodles with chicken, cauliflower, carrots, onions, peppers, and broccoli, prepared with spicy hoisin sauce and cashews. This is a chicken noodle dish with a varied vegetable selection and a cashew topping.

Spice level: mild.

Pad Thai

Price: $17 CAD

Rice noodles with chicken, shrimp, fried tofu, egg, and bean sprouts, accompanied by cashews, scallions, and coriander. This dish includes several protein ingredients: chicken, seafood, tofu, and egg.

Marked gluten-free on the original menu. Includes shrimp, egg, and cashews.

Rice Bowls

Jungle Curry

Price: $16 CAD

A ground chicken curry bowl served with rice, cabbage slaw, corn, and bamboo shoots. Thai basil and makrut lime leaf, listed as kaffir lime leaf on the original menu, provide aromatic herb and citrus notes.

Marked gluten-free on the original menu. Spice level: mild.

Bibim and Bop

Price: $15.50 CAD

A rice bowl with a fried egg, spicy tofu, mushrooms, carrots, bean sprouts, gai lan, and radish. Gochujang, a Korean chili paste, and sesame oil accompany the vegetables, tofu, and egg.

Marked vegetarian on the original menu. Spice level: mild. Includes egg and sesame oil.

General Tao Chicken

Price: $16 CAD

Deep-fried chicken served over rice with cauliflower, broccoli, carrots, and peppers. Sweet chili hoisin sauce accompanies the fried chicken and vegetables. A choice for someone seeking chicken with rice rather than a noodle dish.

Spice level: mild.

Japengo

Price: $15.50 CAD

A plant-based rice bowl with spicy tofu, Japanese eggplant, and bok choy, accompanied by black bean garlic sauce and fried shallots. The dish combines tofu and vegetables with a savoury garlic-based sauce.

Marked vegan on the original menu. Spice level: mild.

Beef Rendang

Price: $17 CAD

Slow-braised beef in a spicy coconut curry sauce, served with rice, broccoli, and fried shallots. This dish uses braised beef rather than fried chicken or sliced pork, with coconut curry as its sauce.

Spice level: mild on the original menu.
